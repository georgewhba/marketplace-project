| PR-01 | P0 | Security | `server/routes/webhooks.routes.ts` | Bosta webhook lacks signature verification. | Attackers can forge delivery events, triggering false commissions and altering financial balances. | Missing HMAC/signature validation logic. | Implement robust signature verification for the Bosta webhook. | OPEN |

| PR-04 | P1 | Finance | `server/routes/admin.routes.ts` (Payouts) | Payout approvals lack strict DB locking. | Two admins approving the same payout concurrently could result in double deduction from vendor balance. | Missing `FOR UPDATE` locks on vendor and settlement records during payout approval. | Implement `db.transaction` with `FOR UPDATE` locks on payouts. | OPEN |
| PR-05 | P1 | Finance | `server/routes/refunds.routes.ts` | Refund lifecycle is financially incomplete. | Refunding delivered items does not properly reverse vendor balance, commissions, and ledger entries. | Refund logic lacks comprehensive financial reversal transactions. | Build a robust, atomic refund pipeline that reverses all financial effects. | OPEN |
